GAME RULES & SAFETY

1. General Safety: Clear all obstacles, trip hazards, and sharp edges from corridors before starting.

2. Movement Speed: Animatronics must walk slowly (toe-to-heel steps). Absolutely NO running or physical tackle moves allowed.

3. Blanket Rule: The Guard wears a light blanket or hoodie over their head to limit peripheral vision to light spots only.

4. Stun Rule: If the Flashlight hits an Animatronic directly, they are frozen for 5 seconds and MUST step back 3 paces.

5. Win Conditions: Guard wins by surviving until 6 AM. Animatronics win if they manage to gently tap the Guard's shoulder.
