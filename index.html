GAME RULES

SECURITY GUARD:

  • Tap FLASH LIGHT to flash. Drains battery!
  • Hold CHARGE MUSIC BOX to keep it above 0%. Pointing music box sends robots back.
  • Tap ROBOT CAUGHT! to gain small flashlight & music box rewards (+15%).
  • Targeted Sabotage: Max 2 uses per game with a 2-minute cooldown!

ROBOTS & STATION COMPUTER:

  • 12-Minute Night: Time is visible on Guard & Station screens!
  • Drainer Mechanics: Loses 1.5% battery per step. Drainer has a separate STEAL POWER button to instantly take 15% from the Guard flashlight!
  • Station Charging: Hold down keys to slowly transfer power to robots in real-time with continuous progress.
  • Sabotage Hazards: Sabotaged Drainer key causes a 50% battery penalty upon use. Sabotaged Crawler/Tank keys lock out for 45s.
