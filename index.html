FULL GAME MANUAL

1. Setup & Blanket Rule: Clear all hallways of hazards. Guard sits in a designated chair with a hoodie/blanket covering their head. Guard MUST look down at their lap unless holding the flashlight.

2. Automatic System Signals: The phone system automatically triggers random MOVE and FREEZE cycles for monsters to prevent cheating.

3. Monster Movement & Noise: Monsters move heel-to-toe (slow walk) ONLY when their screen displays ADVANCE!. Monsters MUST perform their assigned noise cue while moving so the guard hears them approaching.

4. Freeze & Stun Rules: When FREEZE! appears, monsters must stop instantly. If the Guard shines their flashlight on a monster, that monster is stunned for 5 seconds and MUST step back 3 full paces.

5. Sabotage / Hacking: If a monster rolls "HACK MODE", they can approach a room wall or switch and tap SABOTAGE GENERATOR to drain 20% of the Guard's power remotely.

6. Generator Management: The Guard must regularly tap WIND GEN to keep their power from hitting 0%. Balancing winding and checking for monsters with the light is key!

7. Winning the Night: The Guard wins by surviving 6 minutes (until 6 AM). Monsters win if the Guard's generator hits 0% or if a monster successfully reaches out and gently taps the Guard's shoulder.
